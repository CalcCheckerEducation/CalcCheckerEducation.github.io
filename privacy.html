Information CalcChecker stores

CalcChecker has no user account. The app is designed not to save calculator inputs or individual quiz answers. It stores the accepted educational notice, a quiz-completion counter and any active Exam Mode unlock expiry time locally on the device.

Advertising

CalcChecker uses Google Mobile Ads to fund the free app. Google may process device, app and advertising-interaction information. Where required, the app requests advertising privacy choices before ads are made available and provides a privacy-options control in Settings. Google's handling of information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

You can clear CalcChecker's local learning progress in Settings, change available advertising privacy choices in Settings, or remove local app data by uninstalling the app.

Patient information

Do not enter identifiable patient information. CalcChecker is intended for fictional or tutor-provided educational values.

Website hosting

The website hosting provider may process standard technical request logs needed to deliver and protect the website.
